Mesh Gradient gets eight new smooth presets (Peach, Lagoon, Dusk, Citrus, Mint, Aurora, Ink, Basement), a Simple distortion mode with Softness, Warp and Detail, and motion styles (Drift, Breathe, Swirl, Flow) that loop seamlessly over the timeline duration. Passes receive the timeline duration through a new `updateTimelineDuration` hook. Saved gradients keep their exact look: they load in Advanced mode with their Original motion.
